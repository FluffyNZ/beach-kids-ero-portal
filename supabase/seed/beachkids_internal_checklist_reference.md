# Beach Kids internal readiness checklist — source reference

This is the full content of the "Beach Kids – ECE Self-Audit Ready
Checklist" that Beach Kids management supplied. It is Beach Kids' own
practical, internal preparation checklist ("Based on ERO Self-Audit
Checklists, January 2022") — it is **not** the official numbered ERO
document itself (it has no C1/PF1/HS1/GMA1 codes or official ERO
requirement/question wording).

It's kept here because it's exactly the kind of content the brief calls the
"Beach Kids readiness checklist" — the internal sub-items that sit
underneath each official ERO criterion in `beachkids_checklist_items`. Once
the official ERO Self-Audit Checklists document is available and
`ero_criteria` is seeded, each group below should be mapped onto the
relevant official criterion/criteria and inserted as checklist items (a
short script or manual pass through the Beach Kids Readiness Checklist UI
once seeded criteria exist). The first four groups line up with the four
ERO sections; the last four (Staff File Spot Check, Quick Room
Walk-Through, Final Management Check, plus the Audit Pack section) are
Beach-Kids-specific process checks that don't map 1:1 to a single ERO
criterion — consider keeping those as a standalone "Internal Process
Checks" area rather than forcing them under one criterion.

---

## 1. Self-Audit / ERO Pack

- Complete the full Self-Audit Checklist
- Complete the Centre Assurance Statement
- Check every question is answered Yes / No / Unsure
- Record any areas of known non-compliance
- Record what action is being taken and expected timeframe
- Have Service Provider/Contact Person ready to sign
- Have Centre Manager/Person Responsible ready to sign
- Keep supporting evidence organised and easy to locate

## 2. Curriculum

**Curriculum Evidence**
- Current curriculum/programme clearly reflects Te Whāriki
- Current room planning available
- Examples of assessment, planning and evaluation available
- Individual learning stories/portfolios are current
- Evidence children's interests are informing planning
- Evidence whānau and children's life contexts are considered
- Evidence children's preferences and choices are respected
- Evidence of indoor and outdoor learning opportunities
- Evidence of individual and group learning opportunities
- Environment is language-rich
- Evidence of positive and reciprocal kaiako/child relationships

**Culture & Identity**
- Māori as tangata whenua is visible within curriculum and practice
- Te reo Māori is being used within everyday practice
- Te Ao Māori is reflected within the environment/programme
- Te Tiriti o Waitangi is reflected within curriculum practice
- Children's individual cultures and identities are recognised
- Other cultures within the centre are respected and represented

**Positive Guidance**
- Current positive guidance/social competence procedure
- Staff understand how positive guidance is implemented
- Practice observed in rooms matches the written procedure

**Whānau Involvement**
- Evidence whānau aspirations are gathered
- Parents can communicate regularly with kaiako about learning
- Parents receive evidence of their child's learning
- Parents have opportunities to contribute to learning decisions
- Evidence of parent/whānau feedback or consultation

**External Support**
- Records of information/advice sought from outside agencies where applicable
- Relevant SLT/EI/OT/other specialist records accessible where appropriate
- Evidence recommendations are reflected in practice where required

## 3. Premises & Facilities

**Building Documentation**
- Current Code Compliance Certificate/evidence of building compliance available
- Current Building Warrant of Fitness available if required
- Current ECE licence available

**General Environment Walk-Through**
- Indoor spaces allow a range of learning experiences
- Outdoor spaces allow a range of learning experiences
- Quiet spaces are available
- Active play areas are available
- Individual and group spaces are available
- Layout allows effective supervision
- Furniture and equipment are suitable for children's ages and abilities
- Furniture and equipment are in safe condition
- Floors are safe, cleanable and in good repair
- Accessible glass/windows are appropriately protected
- Storage areas are safe and organised
- Hazardous materials are inaccessible to children
- Staff have an appropriate break space
- Private space is available for meetings with parents/staff
- Space is available for planning and curriculum documentation
- Art/paint cleaning facilities are suitable
- Working telephone available
- Lighting is appropriate
- Ventilation is appropriate
- Rooms can be maintained at 18°C or warmer
- Noise levels are reasonable

**Outdoor Area**
- Outdoor area can be safely accessed
- Outdoor surfaces are safe and adequately drained
- Fences are secure
- Gates are secure
- Children cannot leave unnoticed
- Outdoor equipment is safe
- Outdoor area is available for centre use during operating hours
- Under-2s have protected space from more mobile children where required

**Kitchen / Food Areas**
- Children have a safe and hygienic place to eat
- Fridge can maintain perishable food at 4°C or below
- Food storage is hygienic and protected from pests
- Safe means of heating/cooking food
- Appropriate dishwashing facilities
- Hot water available to kitchen sink
- Food preparation surfaces are hygienic and easy to clean
- Children cannot access dangerous cooking appliances unsupervised

**Toilets & Handwashing**
- Sufficient toilets available
- Sufficient warm-water handwashing taps available
- Children's toilets are safely accessible
- Toilet areas are adequately separated from food/play areas
- Hygienic hand-drying system available
- Children's privacy is provided
- Adult toilet available
- Tempering valve/hot-water control working

**Nappy Changing**
- Nappy change area is safe and stable
- Nappy change surface can be hygienically cleaned
- Handwashing facilities are nearby
- Area is separated from food/play areas
- Children's dignity and privacy can be maintained
- Adequate supervision/visibility is possible
- Nappy changing procedure is displayed nearby

**Sick / Soiled Children**
- Suitable facilities available to wash a sick or soiled child
- Written washing/infection-control procedure available
- Designated space available for an unwell child
- Sick child can lie down comfortably
- Sick child can be separated from other children
- Sick child can still be supervised

**First Aid (facilities)**
- First aid kits fully stocked
- First aid kits meet licensing requirements
- First aid kits clearly identifiable
- First aid kits easily accessible to adults
- First aid kits inaccessible to children

**Sleep Areas**
- Cots/stretchers/mattresses are safe
- Sleep surfaces allow children to lie flat
- Shared sleep equipment has cleanable/non-porous surfaces
- Individual clean bedding provided
- Bedding keeps children adequately warm
- Over-2s have suitable rest/sleep space
- Alternative space available for awake children
- Under-2 sleep room/space is appropriate
- Under-2 sleep space allows effective supervision/visibility
- Sufficient cots/beds are available for licensed numbers

**Accessibility**
- Appropriate access/facilities for people with disabilities
- Swimming pool requirements checked or marked N/A

## 4. Health & Safety

**Hygiene**
- Centre premises are clean and hygienic
- Equipment/furniture is maintained in good condition
- Written laundry procedure
- Laundry process is hygienic
- Written nappy changing procedure
- Nappy procedure displayed at nappy area
- Staff practice matches nappy procedure

**Fire & Emergency**
- Current approved Fire Evacuation Scheme available
- Designated assembly areas clearly identified
- Heavy furniture/equipment that could topple is secured
- Current written emergency plan
- Emergency plan covers different emergency scenarios
- Evacuation procedures included
- Emergency roles/responsibilities identified
- Family communication plan included
- Emergency supplies list available
- Emergency supplies actually stocked
- Emergency supplies checked/maintained
- Emergency plan reviewed within the last year
- Improvements from previous reviews recorded
- Emergency drills completed at least every 3 months
- Drill records available
- Drill evaluations completed
- Evidence drill evaluations feed into emergency-plan review

**Sleep Monitoring**
- Sleep monitoring procedure displayed
- Staff understand the sleep procedure
- Children checked for warmth, breathing and wellbeing every 5-10 minutes
- Sleep times recorded
- Every sleep check recorded
- Sleep furniture arranged with safe access
- Adequate airflow around sleeping children
- Sleep equipment and bedding stored hygienically

**Hazard Management**
- Daily hazard checks completed
- Recent completed hazard check forms available
- Hazards identified are followed up
- Written/documented risk-management system
- Accident and incident records are reviewed for recurring hazards
- Cleaning chemicals securely stored
- Medicines securely stored
- Poisons/hazardous substances inaccessible
- Electrical sockets/appliances checked
- Heaters safe
- Kitchen/laundry hazards controlled
- Outdoor area checked for broken glass/animal waste/foreign objects
- Play equipment condition regularly checked
- Windows/glass checked
- Poisonous plant risks considered
- Water hazards considered

**Water Temperatures**
- Children's accessible warm-water taps are no hotter than 40°C
- Hot-water cylinder storage temperature meets the required standard

**Animals**
- Safe/hygienic animal handling practices
- Animals can be appropriately restrained
- Animal areas kept hygienic

**Excursions**
- Excursion procedure available
- RAMS/risk assessment completed for excursions
- Appropriate adult ratio identified
- Ratios never fall below required centre ratios
- First aid requirements covered
- Parent permission available
- Regular excursion permission recorded
- Special excursion permission recorded
- Parents approve proposed excursion ratios
- Person Responsible approves excursions
- Excursion records contain names of children and adults
- Date/time recorded
- Location and travel method recorded
- Risk management recorded
- Ratios recorded
- Person Responsible signature recorded
- Communication system available while off-site
- Written motor-vehicle permission available where applicable
- Appropriate child restraints used where applicable

**Food & Drink**
- Food is served at appropriate times
- Centre-provided food is appropriate in variety and quantity
- Centre encourages healthy eating
- Record kept of food provided by the centre
- Food records retained for at least 3 months
- Food prepared/stored/served hygienically
- Drinking water available at all times
- Older children can access drinking water independently
- Children are seated while eating
- Children are supervised while eating
- Choking-risk foods handled/prepared appropriately
- Parents receive required choking-prevention information at enrolment
- Under-6-month babies/unable-to-drink-independently children are fed semi-upright
- Parent approval exists for food given to infants under 12 months where required

**First Aid Staffing**
- Enough current first-aid-qualified adults are present
- Copies of current first aid certificates available
- Expiry dates have been checked
- First aid coverage maintained during excursions

**Illness, Injury & Incidents**
- Infectious-disease procedure/policy available
- Staff know exclusion requirements
- Unwell children can be separated appropriately
- Parents contacted promptly when children become unwell
- Serious injury/illness procedure available
- Accident/incident records complete (child's name, date/time, description, action taken and by whom, evidence parent was notified/acknowledged)
- Incident records are reviewed and practices changed where required

**Medicines**
- Medicine policy/procedure available
- Written parent authorities available
- Medicine records complete (child's name, medicine and dose, date/time administered, person administering, parent acknowledgement)
- Medicines stored securely
- Expired/unneeded medicine returned or disposed of
- Staff administering medicines have appropriate information/training
- Evidence of medicine training/information retained

**Child Protection**
- Current written Child Protection Policy (covers identification of abuse/neglect, reporting, keeping children safe, procedure for responding to suspected abuse/neglect)
- Child Protection Policy reviewed at least every 3 years
- Staff know the child protection process
- Children protected from inappropriate material
- Alcohol/substance requirements understood and implemented

**Serious Incident Notifications**
- Check whether any incidents required external notification
- Required agency notified where applicable
- Ministry of Education notified where applicable
- Copies of notifications retained
- Mark N/A where no reportable incident has occurred

## 5. Governance, Management & Administration

**Things That Should Be Displayed / Available to Parents**
- Current ECE licence displayed
- Education (Early Childhood Services) Regulations available
- Licensing Criteria available
- Full names and qualifications of staff counting toward qualification requirements displayed
- Complaints procedure displayed/accessible, including how to contact the local Ministry of Education office
- Parents know how to access information about their child
- Parents know how to access centre policies/procedures
- Parents know how to access the most recent ERO report

**Parent Information**
- Current fee information available
- Information about how parents can be involved
- Required information about Ministry of Education funding/expenditure available
- Parents informed about planned policy/review consultation
- Evidence parents have opportunities to contribute to centre policies
- Evidence staff have opportunities to contribute to centre policies

**Philosophy & Internal Evaluation / Self-Review**
- Current centre philosophy, reflects actual centre practice
- Ongoing internal evaluation/self-review process, with schedule/timeline
- Completed review examples, with outcomes/actions recorded
- Evidence changes/improvements have occurred
- Reviews show consideration of relevant NELP priorities

**HR Systems**
- Staff recruitment/selection procedure
- Job descriptions for each role
- Staff induction process, with completed induction evidence
- Regular appraisal/professional-growth system, with PD records
- Serious misconduct definition/process
- Discipline/dismissal procedure

**Children's Worker Safety Checks**
- Written safety-checking procedure
- Every children's worker has a complete safety-check record
- Checks completed before workers have access to children
- Rechecks scheduled/completed every 3 years
- Identity documents sighted/recorded, name-change evidence where applicable
- Police vet considered where required
- Five-year work history obtained
- Teaching Council registration confirmed where applicable
- At least one appropriate referee checked
- Interview and risk assessment completed
- Outcome of safety check recorded, records retained for current employees

**Annual Planning & Finances**
- Current annual centre plan (identifies who/what/when, considers NELP priorities)
- Current annual budget (staffing/leave, PD, equipment/materials, day-to-day operations, maintenance costs included)

**Enrolment Records**
- Current enrolment form for every child: full name, DOB, home address, parent details, emergency/contact details, medical practitioner/medical centre, medical/chronic condition information where applicable, authorised collection people, court orders recorded where applicable
- Previous enrolment records retained for required period, stored securely

**Attendance Records**
- Daily attendance records complete, with arrival/departure times
- Attendance records meet Funding Handbook requirements
- Attendance records retained for required period

**Other Policies / Compliance**
- Immunisation policy/process, required information/records maintained
- Privacy policy/procedures, privacy practices match policy
- Human Rights/EEO requirements reflected in policies/practice
- Policies are on a review schedule

**Teacher Registration / Person Responsible**
- Person Responsible requirements checked
- Practising certificates current
- Teaching Council registrations checked
- Provisionally certificated Person Responsible induction/support documented where applicable
- Staff appointment process reviewed
- Professional development programme reviewed

**Police Vetting**
- Police vets completed for all people requiring them
- Contractor police-vetting requirements checked
- Police vet completed before unsupervised access where required
- Three-yearly police-vet rechecks monitored
- Police vet dates recorded in a central register

**Teaching Council / Provider Notifications**
- Check whether any circumstances required mandatory Teaching Council reporting
- Required notifications made where applicable, copies/evidence retained
- Fit and Proper Person changes reported where required
- Mark N/A where no reportable situation has occurred

## 6. Staff File Spot Check (internal process check)

Physical check of every staff file: signed employment agreement, current job
description, identity evidence, CV/work history, reference check,
interview/recruitment evidence, Children's Worker Safety Check, risk
assessment, police vet/recheck where required, Teaching Council
registration/practising certificate where applicable, First Aid certificate
where applicable, induction completed, appraisal/PD documentation, any
required qualification evidence, relevant visa/work entitlement
documentation where applicable.

## 7. Quick Room Walk-Through (internal process check)

Per room (Tainui, Ohinemuri & Pukewa): daily hazard check completed, ratios
correct, Person Responsible identified, first aid coverage correct, room
temperature appropriate, cleaning products/medicines locked away, nappy/
sleep procedures displayed, sleep records current, first aid kit stocked,
emergency information accessible, exit routes clear, gates secure,
furniture safe, no broken/damaged resources, toilets hygienic, handwashing
facilities stocked, kitchen hygienic, fridge temperature appropriate,
outdoor area hazard-free, children's learning visible, current planning
visible/available, children's cultures/languages represented, te reo
Māori/Te Ao Māori visible in everyday practice, staff can explain key
centre procedures if asked.

## 8. Final Management Check (internal process check)

All centre policies have current review dates; staff know where policies
are located; required procedures match practice; no expired First Aid
certificates, overdue Police vets/safety rechecks or expired practising
certificates; enrolment, attendance, hazard, sleep, medicine,
accident/incident, excursion and emergency drill records complete;
internal evaluation evidence, annual plan, annual budget, philosophy,
parent/staff consultation evidence ready; complaints procedure current;
current licence and required staff qualification information displayed;
any known non-compliance has a documented action plan.

Final readiness sign-off items: Centre Assurance Statement ready to sign;
Curriculum / Premises & Facilities / Health & Safety / Governance,
Management & Administration each marked ready.
